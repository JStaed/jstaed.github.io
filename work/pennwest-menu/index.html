How portions are shown
  • 1 palm is a piece of cooked meat, fish or tofu the size and thickness of your palm, about 3 oz (a deck of cards).
  • 1 cup is about a fist. ½ cup is a tennis ball, ¼ cup a golf ball.
  • 2 tbsp is a ping-pong ball. 1 tbsp is a thumb tip.
  • Pizza is per slice. Sandwiches, wraps, burgers and similar are per whole item. Small items are counted.
  • These are estimates converted from the ounce weights on the dining hall menu. Nutrition values are the dining hall's own.
